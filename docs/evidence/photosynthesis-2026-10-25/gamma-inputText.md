# Main Phase Core Teaching

- Look at the plant in sunlight. Where does its food come from?
- Question 1: A leaf is green. Why is green colour important for making food?
- Discuss with your partner. Then share your reasoning.
- Question 2: A plant kept in a dark cupboard for a week turns pale. What does this tell you about sunlight and food-making?
- Build on the last answer you heard.
- Question 3: Water travels up from the roots. Carbon dioxide enters through tiny leaf pores. How do these two meet to make food?
- Explain the journey in your own words.
- Key idea: Leaves use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to make sugar and release oxygen. This is photosynthesis.

# Extended Task: Photosynthesis

- Your mission: Find out how sunlight affects the green colour of leaves
### Steps:
- Place one potted plant in a sunny spot near a window.
- Place a second similar plant inside a dark cupboard.
- Check both plants every day for three days.
- Write down the leaf colour you see on each plant.
- Show us: Bring your colour notes to class and explain which plant stayed greener
- Skill you'll build: Observe and record changes in leaf colour
- Challenge: Predict what would happen if the dark plant stayed hidden for one week
